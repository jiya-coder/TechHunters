================================================================================
GRAM SABHA IMPLEMENTATION PLAYBOOK & FIELD GUIDE
Forest Rights Act (FRA) Claim Verification Procedure
Ministry of Tribal Affairs & VanDrishti Decision Support System
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
This field playbook provides step-by-step instructions for Gram Sabha Forest Rights 
Committees (FRC) to verify, document, and submit Individual Forest Rights (IFR) and 
Community Forest Rights (CFR) claims under the Forest Rights Act, 2006.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STEP-BY-STEP VERIFICATION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Quorum & Notice
  - Gram Sabha meeting must be convened with at least 50% quorum of all adult members.
  - Mandatory 33% representation of women in Forest Rights Committee (FRC).

Step 2: Claim Filing & Evidence Collection
  - Accept Form A (Individual Claims) and Form B (Community Claims).
  - Admissible Evidence:
    a) Government records, voter IDs, ration cards, census entries prior to Dec 13, 2005.
    b) Physical evidence of cultivation, house structures, boundary marks, trees planted.
    c) Statements of elders, customary rights records, traditional grazing passes.

Step 3: Joint Field Inspection
  - FRC conducts physical field verification accompanied by Forest and Revenue officials.
  - GPS boundary mapping of claimed forest parcel.

Step 4: Resolution & Submission
  - Gram Sabha passes resolution approving or rejecting claims with clear rationale.
  - Submits resolution package to Sub-Divisional Level Committee (SDLC).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ESCALATION & BOTTLENECK RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- If SDLC does not process claims within 60 days, FRC can submit formal appeal to DLC.
- Operational bottleneck tracking active on VanDrishti GIS Platform.
