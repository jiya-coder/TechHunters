District Level Committee (DLC) Guidelines
VanRakshak Decision Support System
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The District Level Committee (DLC), chaired by the District Collector / District Magistrate, 
serves as the final approving authority for the recognition and vesting of forest rights 
under Section 6(5) of the Forest Rights Act, 2006.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. STATUTORY VERIFICATION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) Sub-Divisional Committee Clearance:
    - Ensure SDLC recommendation contains complete joint verification reports.
(2) Revenue & Forest Department Concurrence:
    - Verify land records and forest compartment boundaries.
    - Confirm absence of illegal land alienations or overlapping titles.
(3) Record of Rights (RoR) Entry:
    - Title certificate issued jointly in the name of both spouses (where applicable).
    - Land mutated in revenue/forest records within 30 days of DLC approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ANOMALY DETECTION & WORKFLOW MONITORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- VanRakshak Machine Learning Isolation Forest model flags high rejection rate spikes, 
  pending backlog growth, and SDLC-to-DLC workflow drop-offs.
- District administration can prioritize flagged states/districts using the VanRakshak GIS Map.
