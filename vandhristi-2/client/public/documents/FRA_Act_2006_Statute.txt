================================================================================
THE SCHEDULED TRIBES AND OTHER TRADITIONAL FOREST DWELLERS 
(RECOGNITION OF FOREST RIGHTS) ACT, 2006
[Act No. 2 of 2007]
Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
================================================================================

AN ACT to recognize and vest the forest rights and occupation in forest land in 
forest dwelling Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers who have 
been residing in such forests for generations but whose rights could not be recorded; 
to provide a framework for recording the forest rights so vested and the nature 
of evidence required for such recognition and vesting in respect of forest land.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER I — PRELIMINARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Short title and commencement:
   (1) This Act may be called the Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest 
       Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006.
   (2) It extends to the whole of India.

2. Definitions:
   (a) "Community Forest Resource" means customary forest land within the 
       traditional or customary boundaries of the village or seasonal use of 
       landscape in the case of pastoral communities.
   (b) "Critical Wildlife Habitat" means such areas of National Parks and Sanctuaries 
       where it has been established that such areas are required to be kept as 
       inviolate for the purposes of wildlife conservation.
   (c) "Forest Dwelling Scheduled Tribes" means the members or community of the 
       Scheduled Tribes who primarily reside in and who depend on the forests 
       or forest lands for bona fide livelihood needs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER II — FOREST RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Forest rights of Forest Dwelling Scheduled Tribes and other traditional forest dwellers:
   (1) Individual Forest Rights (IFR): Right to hold and live in the forest land 
       under the individual or common occupation for habitation or for self-cultivation.
   (2) Community Forest Rights (CFR): Community rights such as nistar, right of access 
       to minor forest produce (MFP), fish, water bodies, grazing, and customary 
       territorial jurisdiction.
   (3) Right to protect, regenerate or conserve or manage any community forest resource 
       which they have been traditionally protecting and conserving for sustainable use.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER III — PROCEDURE FOR VESTING OF RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Authorities and Statutory Process:
   - Gram Sabha: Initiates the process for determining the nature and extent of IFR/CFR.
   - Sub-Divisional Level Committee (SDLC): Examines resolution passed by Gram Sabha.
   - District Level Committee (DLC): Final authority to approve and issue titles.
